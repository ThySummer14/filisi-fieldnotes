- [Libbrecht (2005), The physics of snow crystals](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~atomic/publist/rpp5_4_R03.pdf) · 权威综述

- [Libbrecht & Rickerby (2012), Crystal Growth in the Presence of Surface Melting: Novel Behavior of the Principal Facets of Ice](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.5982) · 原始实验作者稿
- [Libbrecht et al. (2015), Toward a Comprehensive Model of Snow Crystal Growth: 4. Measurements of Diffusion-limited Growth at -15 C](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.03389) · 原始实验与半经验模型，arXiv v2
- [Libbrecht (2020), Toward a Comprehensive Model of Snow Crystal Growth: 7. Ice Attachment Kinetics near -2 C](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.06212) · 作者研究报告与半经验模型
- [Bailey & Hallett (2002), Growth Characteristics of Laboratory Grown Ice Crystals Between -20 C and -70 C](https://ams.confex.com/ams/pdfpapers/42237.pdf) · 独立原始会议实验报告
- [Cascajo-Castresana, Morin & Bittner (2021), The ice-vapour interface during growth and sublimation](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/21/18629/2021/) · 独立环境扫描电镜原始实验
