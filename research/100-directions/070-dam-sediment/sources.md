# 来源与范围

[阅读全文](readable.md)

2026-10-07研究文库归档。本文为原创解释与分析，原始研究、观点与示例的适用范围见正文。

- [Kondolf 1997 Hungry Water Effects of Dams and Gravel Mining on River Channels](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002679900048)
  - 类型：学术综述
  - 核对范围：核读原文PDF下切、粗化及条件；学校托管PDF https://people.wou.edu/~taylors/g473/refs/kondolf_97.pdf。
- [Ritchie et al 2018 Morphodynamic evolution following sediment release](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/morphodynamic-evolution-following-sediment-release-worlds-largest-dam-removal)
  - 类型：原始研究摘要
  - 核对范围：核读五年收支；30Mt、65%、90%、60ha均保持原统计对象。
- [USGS 2017 Coastal habitat and biological community response](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/coastal-habitat-and-biological-community-response-dam-removal-elwha-river)
  - 类型：原始研究摘要
  - 核对范围：核读沉积与群落响应，不假定各生物同步受益。
- [Hou et al 2024 Sustained increase in suspended sediments near global river deltas](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-47598-6)
  - 类型：原始研究摘要
  - 核对范围：核读349个三角洲、2000–2020时段和海岸SSC与河流供沙可异向；全文直开受站点跳转限制，未声称通读全文。
- [Fang et al 2023 Substantial role of check dams in sediment trapping](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-023-00728-2)
  - 类型：原始研究
  - 核对范围：核读摘要、结果及方法中遥感识别50226座淤地坝；区分拦蓄估计与黄河同期入海量的比较。
